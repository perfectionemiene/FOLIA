How Folia Works

A simple, intuitive reading workflow built around focus and memories.

1. Upload & Auto-Count

Drop any EPUB or PDF file. Folia automatically extracts metadata and calculates total page counts for you.

2. Highlight & Save Quotes

Select text inside the reader to highlight key passages or click "Save Quote" to keep your favorite lines.

3. Finish & Unlock Memory

Reaching the final page triggers auto-completion, generating a personalized Memory Capsule with your reading stats and quotes.
