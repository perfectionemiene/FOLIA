About Folia

Folia was built on the idea that reading isn't just about counting finished books—it's about preserving who you were when you read them.

Unlike traditional reading trackers that treat your bookshelf like a productivity checklist, Folia provides a calm, intentional space to organize your library, log personal progress, and capture Memory Capsules; snapshots of your thoughts, quotes, and real-life context while reading.

Whether you read two books a year or fifty, Folia helps you look back and see how every story shaped a distinct season of your life.
