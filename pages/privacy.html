Privacy Policy & Terms

Your data remains yours. Plain and simple.

1. Data Storage

Folia processes and saves your library information, reading progress, and personal preferences locally in your browser (via LocalStorage). No personal reading data is transmitted to or sold to third-party tracking servers.

2. Backups & Control

You have total ownership of your data. You can export your full library as a JSON backup or reset local application storage anytime through the Settings page.

3. Terms of Use

Folia is provided as an open tool for personal reading tracking. By using the app, you agree to use it responsibly.
